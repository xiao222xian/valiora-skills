# Poster Themes

## 1. Tech / 科技感

Colors: `#0F172A` (dark navy bg), `#38BDF8` (sky blue accent), `#F1F5F9` (light text)
Style: dark background, neon accents, geometric grid patterns, monospace fonts for dates
Best for: tech meetups, hackathons, developer conferences

## 2. Warm Academic / 暖学术 (LovStudio)

Colors: `#CC785C` (terracotta), `#F9F9F7` (warm cream bg), `#181818` (charcoal text)
Style: warm tones, serif titles, clean layout, subtle paper texture feel
Best for: workshops, book launches, academic talks, cultural events

## 3. Minimalist / 极简

Colors: `#FFFFFF` (white bg), `#000000` (black text), one accent color
Style: lots of whitespace, bold typography, no decorative elements
Best for: art exhibitions, design events, brand launches

## 4. Vibrant / 活力

Colors: gradient from `#FF6B6B` → `#FFE66D`, `#FFFFFF` (white text)
Style: bold gradients, large rounded shapes, playful typography
Best for: community events, festivals, social gatherings, workshops

## 5. Corporate / 商务

Colors: `#1E3A5F` (dark blue), `#FFFFFF` (white), `#E8B931` (gold accent)
Style: clean lines, structured grid, professional typography
Best for: industry conferences, corporate events, formal seminars

## 6. Chinese Traditional / 国风

Colors: `#8B0000` (dark red), `#F5E6CC` (parchment bg), `#2D2D2D` (ink text), `#C5A258` (gold)
Style: traditional patterns, serif CJK fonts, subtle ink wash elements
Best for: cultural festivals, calligraphy events, traditional art exhibitions
