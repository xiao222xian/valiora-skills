## Brand-led landing-page principles

High-quality brand and creator-product pages commonly separate three jobs:

1. establish a memorable belief or possibility;
2. stage real products, artifacts, or creation behavior at meaningful scale;
3. turn understanding into one clear next action.

The reusable lesson is not a specific section order or visual style. It is the
alignment of positioning, headline rhythm, product truth, distinctive form,
proof, and action. Benchmark sites are studied for decision grammar and quality
thresholds, never copied for identity.

The current YouMind homepage remains one useful creator-platform reference for a
creation-led entry, product journey, outputs, proof, and repeated action. Its
words and visual identity are not defaults for this Skill.

- https://youmind.com/

## Image generation

provider at execution time.

## Freshness policy

- Treat model/version names, links, public metrics, badges, pricing, and platform
  rules as time-sensitive.
- Prefer official documentation and the target product's current public truth.
- Record inference separately from verified fact.
- Never turn prepared media, a local package, or an animated counter into proof.
